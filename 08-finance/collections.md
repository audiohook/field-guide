# Collections & Accounts Receivable

> **Department / Section**: 08-finance  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Finance  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Finance, Account Executives  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Twice-monthly AR review with Head of Revenue Ops  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-14

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Collect receivables on time, maintain predictable cash flow, and proactively prevent bad debt write-offs.
- **Definition of Done**: Payments collected within payment terms; overdue accounts resolved or suspended according to the collections policy.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)**: Target for Net 30 <= 45 days and Affiliate <= 120 days.

---

## 3. Collections Procedures
1. QuickBooks will send automated reminders on the due date, 7 days after the due date, and 30 days after the due date.
2. Twice a month, the Senior Accountant and the Head of Revenue Ops meet to review the AR Aging Detailed Report generated by QuickBooks. Total balance owed by customer is also reviewed.
3. As part of the meeting, an action plan is developed to collect on outstanding invoices.
4. If the review yields questions (e.g., duplicative invoice, validity of an old invoice, status of payment), an internal responsible party is identified to resolve the question.
5. All invoices 31+ days over Accounts Receivable can accrue interest.
6. If an edit must be made to an invoice, the Accounting/Finance team will make the adjustment based on applicable standards.
7. If a customer is delinquent past 120 days, a 10-day notice email is sent. If payment is not received within 10 days, the account will be sent to collections.

---

## 4. Campaign Suspension Rule
* Any Net 30 reaching 120 days past due will be reviewed with Head of Revenue for account suspension.
