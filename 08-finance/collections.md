# Collections & Accounts Receivable

> **Department / Section**: 08-finance  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Finance Director  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Senior Accountant, Accounting/Finance Team, Head of Revenue Ops, Head of Revenue  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Twice-Monthly AR Review; Automated Reminders at Due Date, 7 Days and 30 Days Past Due; Notice and Suspension Review at 120 Days Past Due  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-11-18

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Collect receivables on time, maintain predictable cash flow, and proactively prevent bad debt write-offs.
- **Definition of Done**: Payments collected within payment terms; overdue accounts resolved or suspended according to the collections policy.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)**: Target for Net 30 <= 45 days and Affiliate <= 120 days.

---

## 3. Collections Procedures
1. QuickBooks sends automated reminders on the due date, 7 days after the due date, and 30 days after the due date.
2. Twice a month, the Senior Accountant and the Head of Revenue Ops meet to review the AR Aging Detailed Report generated by QuickBooks. The total balance owed by each customer is also reviewed.
3. As part of the meeting, an action plan is developed to collect on outstanding invoices.
4. If the review raises questions (for example, a duplicate invoice, the validity of an old invoice, or the status of a payment), an internal responsible party is identified to resolve each question.
5. All invoices 31+ days over Accounts Receivable can accrue interest.
6. If an edit must be made to an invoice, the Accounting/Finance team makes the adjustment based on applicable standards.
7. If a customer is delinquent past 120 days, a 10-day notice email is sent. If payment is not received within 10 days, the account is sent to collections.

---

## 4. Campaign Suspension Rule
* Any Net 30 reaching 120 days past due will be reviewed with the Head of Revenue for account suspension.
