# Collections & Accounts Receivable

> **Department / Section**: 08-finance  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Finance  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Finance, Account Executives  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Weekly AR Review; Triggered at 30+ Days Past Due  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Collect receivables on time, maintain predictable cash flow, and proactively prevent bad debt write-offs.
- **Definition of Done**: Payments collected within payment terms; overdue accounts resolved or suspended according to the dunning policy.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)**: Target <= 42 days.
* **Overdue AR (> 60 Days)**: < 2% of total open accounts receivable.

---

## 3. Standard Dunning & Collections Schedule (The 20/80 Flow)

| Days Past Due | Action Taken | Primary Owner | Communication |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Day -5** (Prior to due date) | Friendly automated reminder | Automated System | Email with invoice copy |
| **Day +1** | Formal past-due notice | Billing Specialist | Email |
| **Day +15** | Personal phone call & email | Billing Specialist | Direct phone call to AP contact |
| **Day +30** | Account Executive outreach | Account Executive | AE contacts client decision-maker |
| **Day +45** | Formal Demand Letter & Warning | Head of Finance | Written notice: pause warning |
| **Day +60** | **CAMPAIGN SUSPENSION** | Ad Ops / Head of CS | All active DSP campaigns paused |
| **Day +90** | External Collections / Legal | Head of Finance | Account transferred to collection agency |

---

## 4. Campaign Suspension Rule (Non-Negotiable)
* Any client reaching **60 days past due** will have all active campaigns immediately paused in the DSP until past-due balances are settled.
* Only the Integrator has authority to grant a temporary exception.
