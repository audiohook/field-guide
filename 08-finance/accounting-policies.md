# Accounting Policies

> **Department / Section**: 08-finance  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Finance Director  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Senior Accountant, Finance Director, CEO  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Applied at each Monthly Accounting Close; Reviewed at Quarterly Planning  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-11-18

---

## 1. Prepaid Expenses
* Expenses paid in advance for more than $1,500 are recorded as a prepaid expense and amortized over the month(s) to which the expense relates.
* Expenses paid in advance for $1,500 or less are expensed as incurred.

---

## 2. Fixed Assets
The Company expenses fixed asset purchases as incurred when the purchase is less than $2,500. Purchases of $2,500 or more are capitalized.

After 3 years, the Company purchases new equipment for its employees. The old equipment is considered obsolete and is disposed of by the employee at no fee, as it is considered to have a zero dollar salvage value.

---

## 3. Accrued Expenses
All invoices received through Day 4 of the monthly accounting close are reviewed for accrual consideration. In addition, during the weekly call with the CEO, the Finance/Accounting Director requests any additional information on vendors who may require an accrual.

---

## 4. Software Development
All R&D payroll expenses (salaries) are assumed to be related to capitalizable tasks. As a result, 100% of R&D salaries are capitalized on the balance sheet as "Software Development" and amortized over 5 years on a straight-line basis.

---

## 5. Acquisitions
Acquisitions are reviewed by the Accounting Team and are accounted for under FASB ASC 805. Acquisitions are reviewed for, but not limited to, business combination classification (acquisition of a business versus asset purchase), fair market value of assets purchased and identified, appropriate purchase price accounting, and any additional consideration terms.
