# Vendor Payments & Accounts Payable

> **Department / Section**: 08-finance  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Finance / Accounts Payable  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Finance, All Employees Submitting Expenses or Vendor Bills  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Weekly Payment Runs on Thursdays  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Ensure vendors, supply partners, and SaaS providers are paid accurately and on time while enforcing strict internal approval limits.
- **Definition of Done**: Invoices verified against contracted purchase orders, approved by authorized department heads, and paid via scheduled payment run.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **On-Time Vendor Payments**: 100% of vendor bills paid on or before contracted due dates.
* **Approval Compliance**: 100% of paid invoices have documented approval per the matrix.

---

## 3. Spend Approval Matrix

| Spend Amount | Required Approver |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Up to $2,500** | Direct Department Head (budgeted) |
| **$2,501 – $10,000** | Department Head + Head of Finance |
| **$10,001 – $50,000** | Integrator |
| **Over $50,000** | Visionary + Integrator |

---

## 4. Core AP Steps (The 20/80 Flow)
1. **Invoice Intake**: All vendor invoices must be sent directly to `ap@audiohook.com` or uploaded to Ramp/Bill.com.
2. **Coding & Verification**: AP specialist codes expense to appropriate department and GL account.
3. **Approval Routing**: Invoice automatically routed to authorized approver based on the matrix.
4. **Weekly Payment Run (Thursdays)**: Approved invoices paid via ACH, wire, or corporate card.
5. **Remittance Advice**: Confirmation sent to vendor.
