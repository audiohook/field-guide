# 08. Finance

This section documents core financial processes at Audiohook: billing advertisers, managing receivables and collections, processing vendor payments, and maintaining strict accounting controls.

## Accounting Policies

### 1. Prepaid Expenses

* Expenses paid in advance greater than $1,500 will be recorded as a prepaid expense and amortized over the month(s) to which the expense relates.
* Expenses paid in advance less than $1,500 will be expensed as incurred.

### 2. Fixed Assets

The Company expenses fixed asset purchases as incurred (up to $2,500, at which point the expense is capitalized).

At the end of 3 years, the Company purchases new equipment for its employees. The old equipment is considered obsolete and disposed of by the employee at no fee, as it is considered to have a zero dollar salvage value.

### 3. Accrued Expenses

All invoices received through Day 4 of Financial Accounting close are reviewed for accrual consideration. In addition, during the weekly call with the CEO, the Finance/Accounting Director requests any additional information on vendors who may require an accrual.

### 4. Software Development

All R&D payroll expenses (salaries) are assumed to be related to capitalizable tasks. As a result, 100% of R&D salaries are capitalized on the balance sheet as "Software Development" and amortized over 5 years on a straight-line basis.

### 5. Acquisitions

Acquisitions are reviewed by the Accounting Team and are accounted for under FASB ASC 805. Acquisitions are reviewed for, but not limited to, business combination classification (acquisition of a business versus asset purchase), fair market value of assets purchased and identified, appropriate purchase price accounting, and any additional consideration terms.
