# Roadmap: Now & Next

> **Department / Section**: 06-product  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Product  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Leadership, Sales, CS, Engineering  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Reviewed Monthly & Published at Quarterly EOS Planning  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Provide strategic clarity and transparency on where Audiohook is investing its engineering energy, without making artificial date-based promises that sacrifice quality.
- **Definition of Done**: The roadmap is published and visible company-wide using the **Now and Next** framework, with 100% stakeholder alignment before any customer communications.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Cadence Adherence**: Roadmap reviewed and updated monthly in Product/Leadership sync (100% adherence).
* **Now-Commitment Predictability**: >= 85% of items entering "Now" are delivered into production without being demoted.
* **Stakeholder Transparency**: Sales and CS briefed on roadmap updates within 48 hours of publication.

---

## 3. The "Now and Next" Framework (The 20/80 Flow)

Instead of inaccurate 12-month Gantt charts, Audiohook organizes its product roadmap into two clear horizons:

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│                 NOW                  │  │                 NEXT                 │
│  Committed • In Development / Launch │  │  High Confidence • Research / Spec   │
│  "We are actively building this."    │  │  "This is what we tackle after Now." │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘  └──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. The "NOW" Column (Committed & In Progress)
* **Definition**: Initiatives currently staffed, actively being designed, coded, or in final QA.
* **Confidence Level**: High (> 90%).
* **Expectation**: Engineering and Product are actively shipping this work. Scope is locked.
* **Customer Comms**: Sales and CS may share high-level progress with clients as "Currently in active development."

### 2. The "NEXT" Column (Prioritized for Exploration)
* **Definition**: The next 2–4 strategic bets that have survived discovery and customer feedback triage.
* **Confidence Level**: Medium (~70%).
* **Expectation**: Problem framing and PRD drafts are underway, but code is not yet being written. Priorities can shift if business conditions change.
* **Customer Comms**: Shared only as "Under active exploration / on our immediate radar" with NO firm date commitments.

### 3. Later / Backlog (The Opportunity Pool)
* All other ideas, feature requests, and long-term bets live in Linear ideas backlog.

---

## 4. Cadence & Updating Process

1. **Monthly Intake & Prioritization**: Head of Product reviews customer feedback, sales pipeline blockers, and technical debt with Engineering.
2. **Monthly Leadership Review**: Leadership reviews the Now/Next board during monthly strategic sync. Items are promoted from "Next" to "Now" only when an engineering team capacity slot opens up.
3. **Company Bulletin Publication**: The updated roadmap is published internally in `#product-announcements` and reviewed in the next all-hands.

---

## 5. Exceptions & Escalations
* **Emergency Disruptions**: If an executive or client requests inserting an urgent project into "Now", the requester must explicitly name which existing "Now" project will be stopped or delayed to make room.
