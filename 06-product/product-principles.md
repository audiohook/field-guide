# Product Principles

> **Department / Section**: 06-product  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Product  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Product Managers, Designers, Engineers, Leadership  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Applied Daily to Feature Decisions; Reviewed Annually  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Provide consistent decision-making heuristics that help the team make opinionated tradeoffs between speed, simplicity, custom requests, and core platform scalability.
- **Definition of Done**: Product specifications and roadmap priorities explicitly reference and align with our product principles.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Feature Adoption Rate**: >= 60% of target users actively adopting new features within 60 days of launch.
* **Deprecation Discipline**: Sunset features that fall below 5% active usage.

---

## 3. Core Product Principles (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Attribution & Truth Over Vanity**:
   * Advertisers use Audiohook to prove incrementality and ROI. We will never inflate or obscure attribution data to make campaigns look better than they actually are. Trust is our moat.
2. **Speed & Surgical Simplicity**:
   * Build the smallest thing that solves the root problem. Avoid bloatware and unnecessary configuration settings. If 80% of users need an automated default, make it the default.
3. **Solve the Root Need, Not the Exact Feature Request**:
   * When customers ask for a specific button or export, uncover the workflow pain behind it. Build solutions that eliminate the need for manual work rather than adding more buttons.
4. **Platform First, Custom Work Last**:
   * We do not build one-off custom code for individual clients unless it strengthens the generalized platform for our entire ICP.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Strategic client custom feature requests must be vetted through the [Customer Feedback Loop](./customer-feedback.md) and approved in the monthly Roadmap review.
