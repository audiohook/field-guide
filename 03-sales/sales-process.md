# Sales Process Overview

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Strategic Account Executives (SAEs)  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Opportunity enters the Sales Pipeline  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Move each brand through one Asana parent task from lead to signed contract, then hand off cleanly to Client Success.
- **Definition of Done**: Contract signed; CSM introduced; SAE checklist complete; parent task still open for onboarding.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Stage hygiene**: Active opportunities in the correct Asana stage with a named SAE.
* **Handoff SLA**: CSM introduction within 1 business day of signature.
* **Cadence Check**: Weekly Sales & RevOps L10.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

Execute with the [Sales Pipeline Stage Gates checklist](../09-sop-library/checklists/sales-pipeline-gates.md). Asana is the system of action; Clarify mirrors it ([CRM](./crm.md)).

1. **Enter & qualify**: Assign SAE, preserve lead source, create/update Clarify, set Sales Qualified (SQL or Disqualified).
2. **Discover**: Book discovery (Discovery Call Date), run the call, land required notes for a viable proposal.
3. **Collect data**: Finish technical connection + questionnaire subtasks until Asana advances to Proposal Building.
4. **Propose & approve**: Build proposal, link required docs, pass Leadership Review, then client review ([Proposals](./proposals.md)).
5. **Contract & hand off**: Sign IO (+ addenda if required) → [Contract Signed Handoff checklist](../09-sop-library/checklists/contract-signed-handoff.md).

Alternate paths: **Purgatory** (timing) or **Disqualified** (not viable). Never complete the parent task when Sales work ends.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations

| Condition / Trigger | Escalation Path | Notification Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pricing / terms below policy | Head of Sales (written) | `#sales-leadership` |
| Legal redlines | Integrator / Legal | Legal review |
| Duplicate questionnaire task | Merge into questionnaire task | Asana comments |
