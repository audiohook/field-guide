# Lead Qualification & ICP

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: SDRs, AEs  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Evaluated on Every Inbound Lead or First Discovery Call  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Identify high-probability accounts quickly and protect team bandwidth by disqualifying poor-fit leads early.
- **Definition of Done**: Deal is classified as "Qualified Opportunity" or "Disqualified" in CRM with documented reasoning within 24 hours of discovery call.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Lead Response Time**: Inbound leads contacted within 15 minutes during business hours.
* **Disqualification Accuracy**: < 5% of disqualified leads re-entering the pipeline within 90 days.

---

## 3. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) Criteria

A prospect is qualified when they meet all of the following core criteria:

1. **Minimum Budget**: Minimum initial campaign flight test of **$5,000–$10,000**, with potential to scale to **$25k+/month**.
2. **Clear Attribution Model**: Advertiser tracks online actions via pixel, eCommerce conversions (Shopify), app installs, or promo codes.
3. **Target Audience Fit**: Consumer (B2C) or high-volume B2B with an addressable US/Canada audience.
4. **Creative Readiness**: Has existing audio creative (15s/30s/60s) or has budget/appetite for Audiohook creative production.
5. **Decision Maker Access**: Direct contact with Marketing Director, VP Growth, Media Buyer, or Agency Account Director.

---

## 4. Disqualification Signals
* Monthly digital media budget < $5k across all channels.
* Expectation of purely free/unpaid pilot tests.
* Refusal to implement conversion tracking / pixels.
