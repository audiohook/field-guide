# Lead Qualification & ICP

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: SAEs, Affiliate Partnerships, Leadership  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: At each qualification stage, from MQL through LQO  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-14

Current ICP: U.S.-operating Shopify brands with at least 250 eligible weekly conversions.

Full tab: [Audiohook Lead Qualification SOP: Updated Requirements](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1No1SQLkH1yOyvvDE351ZJykgW3_cPy6qirmiFNOA-pI/edit?tab=t.uv3089g71kvj) (updated 2026-10-14).

Audiohook qualifies the advertiser before investing in a proposal, and leadership approves the completed proposal before it is presented to the client.

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Advance only advertisers who fit the current ICP, understand Audiohook's model, and have a leadership-approved proposal.
- **Definition of Done**: The opportunity is SQO only after the mandatory gates below are verified, and LQO only after leadership approves a complete client-ready proposal.

---

## 2. Qualification progression

| Stage | Requirement |
| :--- | :--- |
| **MQL** | Legitimate digital marketing lead demonstrating interest |
| **AQL** | Affiliate-sourced advertiser with documented preliminary fit and relationship context |
| **SQL** | Discovery call booked and accepted by Sales |
| **SQO** | Current ICP verified, Shopify access secured, economics evaluated, targeting and attribution understood |
| **LQO** | Completed client-ready proposal reviewed and explicitly approved by leadership |

---

## 3. SQO gates

A Sales Qualified Opportunity is an SQL where the Strategic Account Executive has verified commercial viability, operational readiness, and alignment with Audiohook's service model. Every gate is required before Leadership approval, unless an explicit exception has been authorized.

| Area | Required standard |
| :--- | :--- |
| Ecommerce platform | Advertiser uses Shopify |
| Geography | Advertiser operates in the United States and has an eligible U.S. conversion market |
| Conversion volume | At least 250 eligible weekly conversions, based on the proposed billable event |
| Measurement access | Shopify collaborator access, including analytics, reporting, order-level records, and customer-event/pixel permissions |
| Conversion event | A clearly defined, trackable billable event, generally a new-customer purchase |
| Commercial economics | Proposed CPA, customer-acquisition economics, and potential campaign volume have been evaluated |
| Tracking feasibility | A supported tracking and reporting configuration has been identified |
| Attribution understanding | Advertiser understands Tuned, Audiohook's performance-based model, and incrementality validation |
| Targeting understanding | Advertiser understands Audiohook's automated targeting, placement, bidding, and optimization |
| Client engagement | Advertiser has participated in meaningful discovery and has a credible path toward a commercial agreement |

GA4 access is no longer a mandatory SQO gate. Shopify access replaces it for the current ICP.

**Website traffic** is supplemental. Document monthly page views when available. The preferred benchmark is 100,000 or more monthly page views. That benchmark is desirable, not mandatory, and missing it does not by itself block SQO or LQO. Capture the number in CRM for later ICP work.

## 4. Targeting education

Before SQO, the SAE covers how Audiohook differs from traditional audio advertising and conventional affiliate marketing. Sending a link or marking a presentation complete is not enough. The advertiser has to show informed acceptance.

* **Automated targeting and placement.** Audiohook's system chooses targeting and placements. Advertisers do not pick podcast shows or direct individual placements as the primary way the campaign is managed.
* **Contextual intelligence.** Audiohook reads podcast content at the episode and segment level: topics, tone, sentiment, and relevance, not only show categories or keywords.
* **Listener-level optimization.** The system uses listener, content, timing, and historical performance signals to find environments likely to convert.
* **Dynamic bidding.** Bidding and allocation move with performance. Campaigns are optimized continuously, not from a static placement list.
* **Geographic targeting.** National, regional, and hyperlocal geography can structure the campaign and the incrementality test. The advertiser does not have to select shows to do that.
* **Division of responsibilities.** Audiohook manages targeting, placement, bidding, and optimization. The advertiser collaborates on conversion objectives, economics, brand requirements, and performance expectations.

Confirm understanding in discovery:

* Are you comfortable letting Audiohook's system determine placements and optimize toward conversions, rather than selecting individual podcasts?
* Do you understand that targeting uses episode- and segment-level context, not only broad show categories?
* Are you aligned with automated bidding and ongoing optimization?
* Are there brand suitability requirements or placement restrictions to evaluate before proceeding?

Record in CRM: Targeting Education Completed, Targeting Model Understood, Automated Placement Model Accepted, brand suitability concerns, and any outstanding targeting objections. Reference: [audiohook.com/targeting](https://audiohook.com/targeting).

## 5. AQL standard

Verified Shopify access and verified conversion volume are not required at AQL. Affiliate Partnerships documents preliminary evidence of:

* Shopify usage
* U.S. operations
* At least 250 eligible weekly conversions
* The partner, agency, or platform relationship
* An appropriate contact or introduction path
* Commercial interest
* Known acquisition objectives and timing, where available

BANT-lite can organize that information. AQL advances to SQL when a discovery call is booked and Sales accepts the opportunity. Sales still verifies the mandatory ICP criteria before SQO.

---

## 6. SQO to LQO

An SQO does not become a Leadership Qualified Opportunity because discovery went well or qualification fields are filled in. A complete, client-ready proposal has to be submitted for leadership review first. Leadership approves the actual presentation intended for the advertiser.

The proposal uses the current approved template, completed by the SAE, and includes:

* Advertiser name and business overview
* Customer-acquisition objectives
* Verified current ICP eligibility
* Eligible conversion event and volume
* Relevant historical business and conversion metrics
* Proposed CPA structure and commercial economics
* Expected conversion volume and campaign opportunity
* Proposed campaign geography and initial testing approach
* Tracking and measurement readiness
* Attribution and incrementality approach
* Targeting and optimization explanation
* Creative strategy and responsibilities, as applicable
* Proposed launch timing
* Material dependencies, limitations, and risks
* Recommended next steps

Leadership reviews ICP eligibility and any exceptions, commercial viability, Shopify measurement readiness, alignment with targeting and attribution, the incrementality approach, operational feasibility and risk, the commercial terms, and the quality of the client-facing proposal.

| Outcome | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Approved** | The opportunity becomes LQO. The approved proposal may be presented. |
| **Revisions required** | Stays SQO until the revisions are completed and approved. |
| **Additional qualification required** | Returns to Sales for more discovery or validation. |
| **Deferred or declined** | Does not advance. |

Sequence: **SQO → Proposal Building → Internal Proposal Review → Leadership Approval (LQO) → Client-Facing Proposal Presentation.**

Record the approving role, date, proposal version or link, and any conditions. Material changes to approved economics, campaign structure, or commitments need renewed approval before they are presented.

## 7. CRM fields

| Field | Expected value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Shopify Confirmed | Yes / No |
| Shopify Access Status | Not Requested / Requested / Approved / Validated |
| Eligible Weekly Conversions | Numeric |
| U.S. Market Eligibility | Confirmed / Not Confirmed |
| Monthly Page Views | Numeric, optional |
| Billable Event | Defined conversion event |
| Targeting Education Completed | Yes / No |
| Targeting Model Understood | Yes / No |
| Automated Placement Model Accepted | Yes / No |
| Attribution Alignment Confirmed | Yes / No |
| Proposal Status | Not Started / In Progress / Ready for Review / Revisions Required / Approved |
| Proposal Link | Document URL |
| Proposal Version | Version or timestamp |
| Leadership Approval Status | Pending / Approved / Conditional / Declined |
| Leadership Approval Date | Date |
| Leadership Approver | User or role |
| Client Presentation Authorized | Yes / No |

CRM controls: SQO requires the mandatory ICP, measurement, targeting, and commercial qualification. Monthly page views are not a blocker. Leadership review requires a completed proposal. LQO requires explicit leadership approval. A client-facing proposal cannot be authorized before LQO. Material commercial revisions require renewed approval. Qualification milestones and approvals stay historically reportable.
