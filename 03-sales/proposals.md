# Proposals & Contracting

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Account Executives  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered when a Prospect Requests a Quote / Media Plan  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Deliver compelling, profitable, and accurate media proposals and execute legally binding Insertion Orders.
- **Definition of Done**: Proposal accepted, Insertion Order signed by authorized signatory, and billing terms confirmed.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Proposal Turnaround SLA**: Proposal delivered within 24 hours of media plan request.
* **Gross Margin Adherence**: 100% of proposals meet minimum gross margin thresholds.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Calculate Inventory & Pricing**: AE models target inventory mix (podcast, streaming, digital radio) and calculates blended CPM ensuring Audiohook gross margin targets are satisfied.
2. **Build Proposal**: Populate standard Audiohook proposal deck with flight dates, targeting parameters, inventory breakdown, and expected impression delivery.
3. **Internal Review (if required)**: Any proposal with custom payment terms, volume discounts, or non-standard performance guarantees requires Head of Sales review.
4. **Generate Insertion Order (IO)**: Generate standard contract via PandaDoc/DocuSign with standard payment terms (Credit Card or Net 30 upon credit approval).
5. **Contract Execution**: Send for signature. Follow up every 48 hours until signed.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Advertisers requesting custom Master Services Agreements (MSAs) or extensive redlines must be submitted to Legal / Integrator for review before signing.
