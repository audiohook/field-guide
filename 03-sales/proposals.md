# Proposals & Contracting

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Strategic Account Executives  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered when Data Collection subtasks are complete (Asana → Proposal Building)  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08  
> **Source**: *Audiohook Proposal Building Process* + *Proposal Review Call Process* + Sales Pipeline stages

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Build an accurate, leadership-approved proposal from collected client data, validate it with the client, and execute contracting documents without creating a new parent task.
- **Definition of Done**: Leadership-approved proposal presented; client agrees to proceed; IO (+ affiliate addendum when required) fully signed and attached to the parent task; stage at **Contract Signed**.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Proposal Completeness**: 100% of opportunities leaving Proposal Building have Proposal Deck, Executive Summary, and Creative Brief linked on the parent task.
* **Leadership Gate**: 100% of client-facing proposals pass Leadership Review before Client Proposal Review.
* **Gross Margin Adherence**: 100% of proposals meet minimum gross margin thresholds (or have written Head of Sales approval).

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Enter Proposal Building**: After Data Collection completes, Asana moves the parent task here. SAE builds from the standard proposal template using questionnaire + discovery notes ([Proposal Building SOP](../09-sop-library/how-to/proposal-building.md)).
2. **Generate Phase 1 GEOs & Validate Fields**: Produce Phase 1 geography targeting. Resolve configuration conflicts and complete proposal-building field validation so downstream automations will route correctly.
3. **Attach Required Parent-Task Links**: Proposal Deck, Executive Summary, and Creative Brief must be linked on the parent task before Leadership Review can complete.
4. **Leadership Review**: Leadership reviews the proposal; SAE verifies all parent-task fields. Approval advances the opportunity; rejection returns for revision.
5. **Client Proposal Review**: Walk the client through the proposal (goals, tracking/billing, geography, commercial terms, prelaunch checklist). Explicitly validate conversion architecture, success metrics, and launch dependencies.
6. **Contracting**: Issue the IO and any billing-dependent addenda (including affiliate-platform addenda when custom fields require them). On full signature → **Contract Signed** and [Sales-to-CS Handoff](./handoffs.md).

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations

| Condition / Trigger | Escalation Path | Notification Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Pricing below CPM / margin floor | Written approval from Head of Sales | Slack `#sales-leadership` |
| Non-standard MSA / legal redlines | Integrator / Legal before signing | Legal review path |
| Missing Phase 1 GEOs or required links | Stay in Proposal Building until complete | Asana parent task |
| Leadership rejects proposal | Return to Proposal Building; do not present to client | Asana + SAE |
