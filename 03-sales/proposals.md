# Proposals & Contracting

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Strategic Account Executives  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Data Collection complete → Proposal Building  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Get a leadership-approved, client-validated proposal signed without leaving the parent task.
- **Definition of Done**: Signed IO (+ required addenda) attached; stage at Contract Signed.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Leadership gate**: 100% of client-facing proposals approved before presentation.
* **Required links**: Deck, Executive Summary, and Creative Brief on every parent task leaving Proposal Building.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

Follow the Proposal Building → Contracting sections of [Sales Pipeline Stage Gates](../09-sop-library/checklists/sales-pipeline-gates.md).

1. **Build** from the standard template with Phase 1 GEOs and validated custom fields.
2. **Link** Proposal Deck, Executive Summary, and Creative Brief on the parent task.
3. **Pass Leadership Review** before any client presentation.
4. **Validate with the client** (goals, tracking/billing, GEO, spend, terms).
5. **Contract** — issue IO (+ affiliate addendum when fields require it) → [handoff checklist](../09-sop-library/checklists/contract-signed-handoff.md).

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations

| Condition / Trigger | Escalation Path | Notification Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Below margin / CPM floor | Head of Sales written approval | `#sales-leadership` |
| Non-standard legal terms | Integrator / Legal | Legal review |
