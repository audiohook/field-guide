# Sales-to-CS Handoff

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales (with 04-customer-success)  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales / Director of Client Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: SAEs, Director of Client Success, CSMs, CSEs  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Contract Signed  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Flip ownership of the **same** parent task from Sales to Client Success in one business day.
- **Definition of Done**: CSM owns the task, advertiser account exists, client introduction sent, onboarding call scheduled.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Handoff SLA**: CSM assigned + introduction within 1 business day.
* **Continuity**: 100% keep the existing parent task (questionnaire merge only exception).

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

Use the [Contract Signed Handoff checklist](../09-sop-library/checklists/contract-signed-handoff.md).

1. **Sign & attach** paperwork; Clarify = Closed-Won.
2. **Confirm automations** multi-homed the task into Onboarding Overview (and tracking/billing routes).
3. **Assign CSM** — ownership transfers; CSE creates advertiser account.
4. **Introduce** — SAE emails client; CSM replies and schedules the onboarding call.
5. **Sales done, task open** — SAE stops active work; parent task continues into [Client Onboarding](../04-customer-success/client-onboarding.md).

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations

| Condition / Trigger | Escalation Path | Notification Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| No CSM in 1 business day | Director of Client Success | `#revops` + Asana |
| No advertiser account before tracking work | CSE | Asana + Slack |
| Duplicate brand task | Merge into questionnaire / continuing parent | Asana comments |
