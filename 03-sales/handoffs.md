# Sales-to-CS Handoff

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales (Collaborative with 04-customer-success)  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales / Head of Customer Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Account Executives, Customer Success Managers  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered Immediately Upon Deal Signing (Closed-Won)  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Seamlessly transfer client knowledge, campaign goals, and technical requirements from Sales to Customer Success so the client experiences an effortless transition.
- **Definition of Done**: Handoff form completed in CRM, internal handoff meeting held, and joint kickoff email sent to client within 24 hours.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Handoff SLA**: Kickoff email sent within 24 hours of signed contract.
* **Data Completeness**: 100% of handoff fields completed (no missing pixel or creative specs).

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Complete Closed-Won Checklist in CRM**: AE documents:
   * Target audience parameters and geo-targeting.
   * Primary KPI (e.g., CPA target, ROAS target, website traffic).
   * Creative status (existing assets vs. Audiohook production).
   * Tracking setup (Shopify app, pixel, UTMs).
2. **Assign CSM**: Head of CS assigns dedicated Customer Success Manager based on category and bandwidth.
3. **Internal Handoff Sync (15 mins)**: AE and CSM review client context, personalities, expectations, and any sensitive promises made during the sales cycle.
4. **Introductory Email**: AE sends warm introduction connecting client to CSM, attaching kickoff scheduling link.
5. **CS Takes Ownership**: CSM leads the client onboarding and kickoff call.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* If critical campaign setup assets (creative or pixel implementation) are delayed by the client, CSM initiates the escalation protocol outlined in [Client Onboarding](../04-customer-success/client-onboarding.md).
