# CRM Hygiene & Pipeline Management

> **Department / Section**: 03-sales  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Sales / Revenue Ops  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All Sales Team Members  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Daily Updates; Inspected Weekly at Sales L10  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08  
> **Source**: *Audiohook Sales Pipeline Asana Workflow* + Clarify sync standards

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Keep Asana (system of action) and Clarify (CRM / forecast) synchronized so every opportunity has an accurate stage, owner, value, and next step.
- **Definition of Done**: Every active opportunity has matching Asana Kanban stage and Clarify stage, updated close date / expected value, documented next step, and no stale activity > 14 days.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Forecast Accuracy**: Closed revenue within ±10% of monthly sales forecast.
* **Pipeline Hygiene**: Zero active deals with "No Activity" > 14 days.
* **Asana ↔ Clarify Sync**: Stage mismatches cleared within 1 business day of Asana movement.

---

## 3. Standard Stages (Asana ↔ Clarify)

Asana owns operational stage. Clarify owns forecast probability and reporting. Update Clarify when the Asana stage changes.

| Asana Kanban Stage | Clarify Stage (recommended) | Exit Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Lead Entry / pre-SQL | Lead / Inbound | SAE assigned; advancing toward SQL or Disqualified |
| **SQL** | Qualified Opportunity | Discovery Call Date set |
| **Discovery / Meeting Booked** | Qualified Opportunity | Discovery call occurs (date arrives) |
| **Data Collection** | Qualified Opportunity | Required data + technical connection subtasks complete |
| **Proposal Building** | Solution Presented / Proposal Prep | Deck, Phase 1 GEOs, Executive Summary, Creative Brief linked; fields validated |
| **Leadership Review** | Proposal Internal Review | Leadership approval + parent-task field verification |
| **Client Proposal Review** | Proposal Sent | Client agrees to proceed to contracting |
| **Contracting** | Contracting / Verbal | All parties sign IO (+ affiliate addendum if required) |
| **Contract Signed** | Closed-Won | Onboarding Overview multi-home complete; CSM path started |
| **In Onboarding** | Closed-Won (handoff complete) | SAE introduction done; SAE work complete — parent task stays open |
| **Purgatory** | On Hold / Nurture | Future follow-up task + expected date set |
| **Disqualified** | Closed-Lost | Loss / DQ reason documented in Clarify |

---

## 4. Mandatory Fields

### Asana parent task
* Owner (SAE until CSM assignment at Contract Signed)
* Lead source / External Lead Source / referring partner (when applicable)
* Discovery Call Date (when booked)
* Sales Qualified (Yes/No)
* Technical and billing custom fields required for downstream automation
* Links: Proposal Deck, Executive Summary, Creative Brief (before Leadership Review completes)

### Clarify deal
* Deal Amount (expected gross media spend)
* Close Date (realistic; never left in the past)
* Next Step (explicit action + date)
* Stage aligned to the table above
