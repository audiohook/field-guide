# Audiohook Company Field Guide

Welcome to the **Audiohook Company Operator's Manual**. This repository serves as our centralized company operating system based on the **Entrepreneurial Operating System (EOS)**. It documents the core processes that drive Audiohook across all business functions.

## 📖 How to Use & Edit This Handbook

1. **Read in GitHub**: Click any section below to browse processes directly in your browser.
2. **Edit in Place**: Found a process that needs updating? Click the **pencil icon** (`Edit this file`) at the top right of any page.
3. **Propose & Review**: Make your changes, preview them, and click **"Propose changes"** to open a pull request for your department lead.
