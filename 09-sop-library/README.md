# 09. SOP Library

The **Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Library** stores task-level, click-by-click instructions, screenshots, and operational checklists that support our core EOS processes.

---

## The EOS Difference: Core Process vs. SOP

* **Core Process (Sections 01–08)**: High-level overview of the 20% of steps that produce 80% of results. Defines the sequence, accountability seat, measurables, and escalation paths. Followed by everyone.
* **SOP & Checklists (Section 09)**: Granular, task-specific, tool-by-tool instructions. When someone asks *"Which exact dropdown do I click in the DSP to set up an audio pixel?"*, they consult an SOP in this library.

---

## Library Structure

* 📂 **[How-To Guides](./how-to/README.md)**: Detailed tactical how-to walkthroughs organized by department and tool.
* 📋 **[Checklists](./checklists/README.md)**: Reusable pre-flight, launch, QA, and audit checklists.
