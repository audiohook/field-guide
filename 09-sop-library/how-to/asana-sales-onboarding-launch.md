# How To: Asana pointer for Sales → Onboarding → Launch

> **Department / Section**: 09-sop-library / how-to  
> **Supports**: Sales + CS core processes  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08

Day-to-day work lives in the **checklists**, not in this page. Use this only for the mental model and where to click conceptually.

---

## Rules that never change

1. **One parent task** per brand from lead through Incrementality completion (questionnaire merge is the only duplicate fix).
2. **Multi-home** into specialist projects; do not copy the parent.
3. **Asana = action**, **Clarify = forecast**. Update Clarify when Asana moves.
4. **Due dates = expected completion**, kept honest by the assignee.
5. **Do not complete the parent task** at Sales handoff or at go-live — only after Incrementality Testing.
