---

## Before the call

- [ ] Confirm the parent task is in **Discovery / Meeting Booked** with an accurate Discovery Call Date
- [ ] Review available brand context (site, questionnaire if submitted, referral/MQL notes, Clarify)
- [ ] Prepare to show you did homework — do not open with a generic pitch
- [ ] Block time immediately after the call for structured notes (no heavy note-taking during the call)

---

## Mindset

Listen first. Map how they acquire customers today, where audio fits, and what success means in their economics — then play the business back to them. The goal is proposal readiness, not a product tour.

---

## Call flow (critical path)

1. **Open with homework** — Prove you understand their brand, category, and likely acquisition model.
2. **How they acquire customers today** — Channels, partners, funnel ownership.
3. **History with audio** — Prior podcast/streaming tests, what worked or failed.
4. **Map the customer journey** — Awareness → consideration → conversion → retention touchpoints.
5. **Measurement** — GA4, Shopify, pixels, affiliate platforms, promo codes; known leakage.
6. **Economics** — CPA vs CAC, contribution margin, what “efficient” means for them.
7. **Define success** — Explicit targets Audiohook will be judged against.
8. **Geographic reality** — Where they can and cannot sell; Phase 1 GEO implications.
9. **Seasonality & launch realities** — Flight constraints, creative lead times, tech dependencies.
10. **Creative environment** — Existing assets vs Audiohook production; brand constraints.
11. **Decision & implementation process** — Stakeholders, legal, tracking owners, timeline.
12. **Close by playing it back** — Summarize their business, success definition, and proposed next step (data collection / questionnaire / technical connection).

---

## After the call (proposal readiness notes)

Capture structured notes covering:

| Area | What to lock |
| :--- | :--- |
| Business & objective | Why audio now; primary goal |
| Conversion | Events that define success |
| Attribution & leakage | Known gaps vs Tuned / platform truth |
| Economics | Target CPA/CAC and spend envelope |
| Marketing mix | What else is running |
| Measurement | Access path (GA4 / Shopify / other) |
| Audio history | Prior tests and lessons |
| Success | Numeric targets + timeline |
| Campaign constraints | GEO, seasonality, creative, brand rules |
| Stakeholders | Decision-maker + implementation owners |

### Proposal readiness test

Do not advance out of Data Collection until you can answer: what success is, how it is measured, economics that make the media plan viable, Phase 1 GEO logic, and who must approve contracting and tracking.

---

## Asana / Clarify

* When the Discovery Call Date arrives, Asana moves the parent task to **Data Collection**.
* Complete technical connection and questionnaire subtasks there.
* Update Clarify next step and notes the same day.
