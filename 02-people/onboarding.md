# Onboarding Logistics & Setup

> **Department / Section**: 02-people  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: People Ops / Office Manager  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: People Ops, IT Admin, Hiring Managers  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered 14 Days Prior to New Hire Start Date  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Ensure all hardware, software accounts, benefits, and paperwork are completed before a new hire's first day so they can focus on learning.
- **Definition of Done**: All systems provisioned, hardware delivered, tax/payroll forms completed, and Day 1 calendar invitations scheduled.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Day 1 Readiness**: 100% of logins and hardware operational on Day 1 by 9:00 AM local time.
* **Paperwork Compliance**: 100% of I-9 / W-4 / direct deposit forms submitted within 3 business days.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Offer Signed & Profile Created**: Add new hire to Gusto / payroll and initiate background check.
2. **Hardware Order (10 Days Prior)**: Order company laptop and accessories; ship to employee's address with tracking.
3. **Account Provisioning (3 Days Prior)**: Create Google Workspace email, 1Password vault, Slack account, and department tool invites (Linear, GitHub, HubSpot).
4. **Day 1 Welcome Call (Day 1, 9:30 AM)**: People Ops reviews benefits enrollment, payroll setup, and company policies.
5. **Manager Hand-Off**: Transition new hire to hiring manager for the [Company Onboarding Program](../01-company/onboarding.md).

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Hardware delivery delays: Ship backup device or authorize local equipment purchase with expensed reimbursement.
