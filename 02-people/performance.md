# Performance Reviews & EOS People Analyzer

> **Department / Section**: 02-people  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Integrator / Department Leads  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All Managers and Employees  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Quarterly (5-5-5 Check-ins) & Annual Review  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Foster continuous improvement, ensure alignment with Audiohook Core Values, and verify that every team member is in the Right Seat with high GWC.
- **Definition of Done**: 100% of team members receive structured quarterly feedback via the 5-5-5 framework, with clear goals for the next quarter.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Review Completion Rate**: 100% of quarterly reviews completed within 14 days of quarter end.
* **Scorecard Achievement**: >= 80% of quarterly individual rocks achieved.

---

## 3. The 5-5-5 Quarterly Review Cadence

Every quarter, managers and team members hold a dedicated 60-minute conversational check-in covering:

1. **5 Core Values**: Review each core value using the People Analyzer (+, +/-, or -).
   * **+**: Exhibits the value consistently.
   * **+/-**: Sometimes exhibits, sometimes doesn't. Needs coaching.
   * **-**: Does not exhibit the value. Unacceptable over time.
2. **5 Core Roles (GWC)**:
   * **Gets It**: Do they deeply understand their seat? (Yes/No)
   * **Wants It**: Do they genuinely want to do the work? (Yes/No)
   * **Capacity**: Do they have the skill and time to execute? (Yes/No)
3. **5 Quarterly Rocks**: Review progress on previous quarter's rocks and align on rocks for next quarter.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Performance Management
* **The "Three Strike" Process**: If an employee receives a "-" or fails GWC:
  1. *Strike 1 (Awareness)*: Documented conversation identifying the gap; 30-day coaching plan.
  2. *Strike 2 (Formal PIP)*: Written 30-day Performance Improvement Plan.
  3. *Strike 3 (Offboarding)*: If unresolved after 30 days, transition employee out respectfully.
