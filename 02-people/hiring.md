# Hiring Process & Rubrics

> **Department / Section**: 02-people  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of People / Hiring Manager  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Hiring Managers, Interviewers, Recruiters  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered on Approved Job Requisition  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Execute an efficient, fair, and rigorous recruiting process that evaluates candidate competencies and core values alignment.
- **Definition of Done**: Candidate completes full interview panel, interview scorecard submitted within 24 hours of each round, and offer extended to top candidate.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Time to Fill**: Role filled within 45 days of job posting.
* **Scorecard Completion SLA**: 100% of interview rubrics submitted within 24 hours of interview.
* **Candidate NPS**: Average candidate interview experience rating >= 4.5/5.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Requisition & Scorecard Creation**: Hiring Manager writes Job Scorecard defining the role outcomes, competencies, and gets budget approval from Integrator.
2. **Sourcing & Application Review**: Screen inbound applications and active outreach against scorecard requirements.
3. **Screening Call (30 mins)**: Screen candidate on basic qualifications, career goals, and compensation range.
4. **Technical / Functional Assessment (60 mins)**: Practical work-sample test evaluated against a standardized rubric.
5. **Team & Core Values Interview (45 mins)**: Behavioral interview using the EOS People Analyzer to evaluate core value fit.
6. **Debrief & Reference Checks**: Panel meets for a 15-minute sync; hiring manager calls 2 references.
7. **Offer Approval & Extension**: Offer generated, approved by Integrator, and presented to candidate.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Any disagreement among interviewers on cultural fit results in a "No Hire". 
* Counter-offers or salary requests exceeding approved band must be approved by the Integrator.
