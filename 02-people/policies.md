# Company Workplace Policies

> **Department / Section**: 02-people  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: People Ops / Integrator  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All Employees  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Continuous; Reviewed Annually  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Establish clear, respectful expectations for flexible paid time off, remote working, expense reimbursements, and workplace standards.
- **Definition of Done**: Policies are understood and adhered to by all team members with zero compliance violations.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Minimum Time Off Encouraged**: Every employee takes at least 15 days of PTO annually to prevent burnout.
* **Expense Submission SLA**: Business expenses submitted within 30 days of occurrence.

---

## 3. Core Policies (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Flexible Paid Time Off (PTO)**:
   * We treat team members as adults. Take the time you need to recharge while ensuring your responsibilities and accounts are covered.
   * Request planned PTO >= 2 weeks in advance in Gusto; notify your team via Slack.
2. **Remote & Hybrid Work**:
   * Audiohook is remote-first. You are trusted to manage your schedule and deliver on your commitments.
   * Maintain core overlapping availability between 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM Mountain Time for collaborative meetings and L10s.
3. **Equipment & Home Office**:
   * Company laptop provided. A one-time home office setup stipend is provided to full-time employees upon hire.
4. **Professional Conduct & Anti-Harassment**:
   * Zero tolerance for discrimination, harassment, or retaliation. Report any concern immediately to People Ops or the Integrator.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Extended leaves (medical, parental, bereavement) should be coordinated directly with People Ops.
