# Compensation Framework

> **Department / Section**: 02-people  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Integrator / Head of People  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Leadership, Hiring Managers  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Reviewed Annually; Applied During Hiring & Promotions  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Provide competitive, transparent, and equitable compensation that attracts and retains top-tier talent while sustaining healthy company unit economics.
- **Definition of Done**: Every role is benchmarked to verified market data, with established salary bands and objective variable compensation formulas.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Market Competitiveness**: Salaries benchmarked to 75th percentile of comparable tech/ad-tech companies.
* **Internal Equity**: Zero unjustified pay discrepancies across equivalent roles and performance levels.

---

## 3. Core Compensation Pillars (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Base Salary Bands**: Structured by level (Associate, Mid, Senior, Lead, Director) based on market survey data. Bands are updated annually.
2. **Variable Compensation (Sales & CS)**: Commission and bonus plans based on clearly measurable outcomes (Closed Revenue, Net Revenue Retention). Plans are documented before each calendar year.
3. **Equity Incentive (Options)**: Stock option grants aligned with long-term enterprise value creation, subject to standard 4-year vesting with a 1-year cliff.
4. **Annual Compensation Review**: Conducted every Q4 in tandem with EOS annual planning, taking effect January 1.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Out-of-band salary or equity offers require explicit sign-off from the Integrator and Visionary.
