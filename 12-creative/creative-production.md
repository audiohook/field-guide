# Creative Production

> **Department / Section**: 12-creative  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Creative Director  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Creative  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: A client needs new scripts and ads (a launch or a creative refresh)  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-26

Reference pages linked from the steps are outlines until Creative writes them.

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Turn client research into approved scripts, then into produced ads with voices and music, uploaded for review.
- **Definition of Done**: Every approved script has been produced with its voice and music variants and uploaded for review.

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Client research** — Creative gathers what this round of scripts needs to reflect for the client and records it in [Client Creative Notes](./client-notes.md).
2. **Select messaging types** — Creative chooses the messaging types for this round from [Messaging Types](./messaging-types.md).
3. **Write scripts** — Creative writes each script from the [Script Template](./script-template.md). When Claude drafts or revises a script, brief it using [Briefing Claude for Scripts](./briefing-claude.md). Name the files with [Script and Ad File Titling](./file-titling.md).
4. **Get script approval** — Creative submits the scripts and waits for approval before any production. A script that is not approved goes back to step 3 with the review notes.
5. **Produce voices and music** — Creative produces every approved script with its different voices and music. After a voice is selected, mix it using [Post-Voice Selection Mixing](./mixing.md) and [Creative Audio Best Practices](./audio-best-practices.md). When ElevenLabs blocks the work, use [ElevenLabs Troubleshooting](./elevenlabs.md). Name finished files with the titling rules.
6. **Upload for review** — Creative uploads the produced ads for review and approval. Notes that change a script go back to step 3. Notes that change a voice, mix, or music bed go back to step 5.

When the ads are approved, Rev Ops takes the files through [Creative Management](../05-ad-operations/creative.md).
