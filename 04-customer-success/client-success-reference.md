## Account status

* **Onboarding**: New client, never been active, in the process of going live.
* **Active**: Live.
* **Paused**: A temporary pause. Examples: a billing issue they need to clean up, or a pause of a couple of weeks with a clear return date or a scheduled meeting to come back. Use this when there is 50% or more confidence they will restart. In the UI, billing conversions stop 30 days after the account is paused.
* **Restarting**: Was paused, and is now moving back to live, with steps laid out and a go-live date.
* **Cancelled = Churned**: No date or plan to come back. They do not want to continue and the account is in save-it mode, or Audiohook chose not to work with them. If the account was active, move the Clarify deal stage to cancelled churned. If it was canceled during onboarding, move the deal stage to "canceled in onboarding." In Clarify, move the company status to canceled. In the UI, change status to canceled and set the last billable date.

## Fillable budget

* If fillable budget equals their budget, they are budget capped and unwilling to increase. Something else is blocking scale.
* If fillable budget is higher than their budget, they are open to increasing if spend goes over. Choose a number they would realistically be willing to increase to.
* If fillable budget is lower than their budget, something other than budget is blocking scale. Choose a number close to their monthly spend.

## Campaign types

* **Contextual Feeder** — 1 impression from a podcast. The first touch is on a podcast. Used for retargeting on frequency campaigns.
* **Frequency** — 5 impressions. Used to retarget feeder campaign traffic.
* **Affinity Feeder** — 1 impression from a podcast. The first touch is on the client website, then an impression is delivered when they listen to a podcast, then they are retargeted through the frequency campaign.

## How to approach a conversation

1. **Acknowledge** — Recognize the client's concern and thank them for bringing it forward.
2. **Empathize** — Show understanding without becoming defensive. Understanding and patience only, never frustration.
3. **Clarify** — Restate the issue to confirm understanding and add any necessary context.
4. **Describe actions or solutions** — Explain what is being done, what is available, or what the next steps are.
5. **Reassure** — Reinforce the relationship and confidence in resolving the issue.
6. **Invite continued dialogue** — Ask for follow-up questions or more feedback.

## How to identify a geo to target

The Client Success Engineer runs the account through the Meta or Stella analysis tool.

## Quality score

The Audiohook algorithm scores conversions with:

* **Impression count** — More impressions, higher score.
* **Time to conversion** — More recent, higher score.
* **UTM the conversion came in on** — Organic scores higher than paid media.
* **Returning users** — Lower score.

Impression timestamp is when the podcast was downloaded.
