# Campaign Optimization

> **Department / Section**: 04-customer-success  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Customer Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: CSMs, Media Buyers  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Weekly Routine for Every Active Flight  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-14

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Maximize advertiser ROI and CPA performance by systematically trimming underperforming inventory and scaling top-performing channels.
- **Definition of Done**: Weekly performance audit completed, optimization actions applied in DSP, and documented in client notes.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Client CPA / ROAS Goal Attainment**: >= 80% of active campaigns achieving target CPA/ROAS.
* **Pacing Accuracy**: Final flight delivery within 98%–100% of contracted budget.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Weekly funnel metrics check**:
   * Complete the [Funnel Metrics | 30–60 Day Comparison template](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1GcZHQNn13XwBrVXA08kvcnVCUZOpq-Ou/edit?gid=126154599#gid=126154599) for every active flight before changing bids or inventory.
2. **Review Performance Against Benchmark**:
   * Analyze conversion rates by publisher, show category, device, and time of day.
3. **Prune Underperforming Inventory**:
   * Blacklist low-converting shows, apps, or exchanges that have burned > 3x target CPA without a conversion.
4. **Scale Winners**:
   * Shift budget allocations to top-converting dayparts, geolocations, and podcast genres.
5. **Creative Refresh Check**:
   * Check audio frequency. If average frequency > 5 per user, recommend creative variation to prevent listener fatigue.
6. **Bid Price Adjustment**:
   * Adjust max bid thresholds to optimize win rates during high-converting hours.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Performance > 50% worse than target CPA after 14 days: Trigger [Escalations & At-Risk Accounts](./escalations.md).
