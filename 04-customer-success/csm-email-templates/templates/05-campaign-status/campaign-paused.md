# Campaign Pause Confirmation

**When to use:** Confirm a campaign pause and remind the client of the 30-day attribution window.

---

Confirming that the **[Brand Name] campaign was paused effective [Pause Date]**.

As a reminder, conversions can continue to be attributed for up to **30 days following the pause date** based on prior ad exposure. [Brand Name] will remain responsible for any conversions attributed during this window.
