# Attribution Explanation

**When to use:** Client asks how Audiohook / Tuned™ attributes conversions.

---

Hi [First Name],

Happy to provide some additional context on how attribution works with Audiohook!

Audio is different from channels like paid search or social because it's inherently **clickless**. Someone may hear an ad and later return through search, direct traffic, or another channel to make a purchase, meaning traditional last-click attribution can miss audio's role in the customer journey.

That's why we built **Tuned™, our audio-specific attribution engine**. At a high level, Tuned connects:

**Ad Exposure → Site Activity → Conversion**

We know when a listener is served an Audiohook ad, and our site integration captures subsequent visits, key actions, and conversions. Tuned then evaluates the full customer journey to determine whether Audiohook meaningfully influenced that conversion.

Importantly, **we don't attribute every conversion that occurs after an ad impression**. Tuned evaluates signals including:

- **Recency:** How much time passed between exposure and conversion
- **Frequency:** How many Audiohook impressions the listener received
- **Engagement:** Their on-site behavior following exposure
- **Pathing:** Other channels and touchpoints involved in their journey

The goal is to identify conversions that were **highly unlikely to have occurred without Audiohook's influence**, rather than simply taking credit based on exposure alone.

For transparency, every attributed conversion is also tied to a unique order ID, allowing you to reconcile Audiohook conversions directly against your own records.
