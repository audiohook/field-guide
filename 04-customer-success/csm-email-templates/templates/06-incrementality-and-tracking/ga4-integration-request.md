# GA4 Integration Request

**When to use:** Ask the client to connect a read-only GA4 integration in the Audiohook dashboard.

**Subject:** [Brand Name] | Audiohook + GA4 Integration

---

Hi [Brand Rep],

Hope you are well! Would **[Brand Name]** be willing to connect a **read-only** integration with GA4 in the Audiohook dashboard? Here are some of the pros:

- This allows us to recognize and understand if/when there is a break in tracking so we can fix it quickly.
- This allows Tuned, Audiohook's attribution model, to focus on the right places. This means better, faster optimization.
- <u>This is read-only access,</u> we cannot make any changes. Our existing agreements already state that we will keep this information private and only use it for internal strategy and support.
- More efficient incrementality testing

Can we get this connected this week? It's a simple flow:

1. Log in to the [dashboard](http://console.audiohook.com/) and navigate to your menu in the top right corner
2. Select Integrations
3. Select GA4
4. Connect
5. Confirm with me when it's done!

Let me know if you have any questions!

Warmly,

[CSM]
