# Incrementality Test Kickoff

**When to use:** Kick off an incrementality study and request the initial 150-day conversion file.

---

Hi [Client Name],

We're excited to get your incrementality test underway! This study will give us an independent view of the impact Audiohook is having on your business, and we're looking forward to sharing the results with you.

To get started, we'll need a file with the last 150 days of conversion data across all channels. Please include one row per conversion with only the following three columns and no PII.

1. Date of the conversion
2. State where the conversion occurred. Please use the order location, not the shipping location.
3. Revenue amount of the conversion

Also, please call out any confounders that would create unusual spikes or valleys in the data, such as big promotions/sales, or seasonality.

Once we receive the file, we'll confirm everything is in the correct format and kick off the analysis.

If you have any questions about the data requirements, just let me know. We're excited to get started!

Warmly,

[CSM]
