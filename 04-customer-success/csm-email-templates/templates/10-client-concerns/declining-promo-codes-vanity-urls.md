# Declining Unsupported Request — Promo Codes / Vanity URLs

**When to use:** Client asks to use a promo code or vanity URL in the creative.

---

Hi [Client Name],

Thanks for asking! While we understand the value of promo codes and vanity URLs in some channels, Audiohook does not use them in our creative.

Because audio is often consumed while driving, working, or multitasking, asking listeners to remember and later enter a specific code or URL creates unnecessary friction. More importantly, these methods tend to significantly undercount audio-driven conversions because they only capture customers who take that specific action.

Instead, Audiohook uses more sophisticated attribution methods designed to measure conversions without requiring any additional action from the listener. This allows us to capture the broader customer journey, including listeners who hear an ad and later search for the brand or purchase through another path.

For those reasons, we'll keep the CTA simple and direct listeners to [Brand/Website] without a campaign-specific promo code or vanity URL.

If you'd like to read more about why we take this approach, we have a helpful overview here: [Why We Don't Rely on Promo Codes and Vanity URLs at Audiohook](https://audiohook.substack.com/p/why-we-dont-rely-on-promo-codes-and)

Thanks!

[CSM]
