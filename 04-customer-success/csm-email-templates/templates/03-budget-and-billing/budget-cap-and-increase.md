# Budget Nearly Exhausted / Budget Increase Recommendation

**When to use:** Campaign is nearing or has reached its monthly budget cap; recommend additional budget.

---

Hi [First Name],

I wanted to send a quick budget update — [Brand Name] is [nearing / has reached] its monthly budget cap of **$[X]**. So far, the campaign has driven **[X] conversions and $[X] in revenue at a [X]x ROAS**.

At the current pace, we [expect to reach the cap by X / have reached the cap and will need to pause the campaign].

We're seeing strong performance and would love the opportunity to keep the momentum going. Based on current results, an additional **$[X] in budget** will generate **[X] additional conversions / $[X] in incremental revenue**.

We'd love to continue scaling while performance is strong and avoid an extended pause in delivery as this can have negative effects on campaign volumes moving forward.

Warmly,

[CSM]
