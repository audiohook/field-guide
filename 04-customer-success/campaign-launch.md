# Campaign Launch

> **Department / Section**: 04-customer-success  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Director of Client Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: CSMs, CSEs  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Parent task at Take Live with green readiness checklist  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Turn a ready campaign on, tell the client and the company, and hold In Testing until Incrementality finishes.
- **Definition of Done**: Live campaign; notices sent; In Testing; parent task completed only after Incrementality Testing.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **On-time launch** when Take Live criteria are met.
* **T+4h pacing check** on every launch.
* **Cadence Check**: Weekly CS L10.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Confirm readiness** — [Take-Live Readiness checklist](../09-sop-library/checklists/take-live-readiness.md) fully green (or N/A).
2. **Activate** — Launch in Audiohook UI; confirm bidding.
3. **Notify** — Client email + dashboard access; Slack announcement; Accounting for promos/credits/affiliate billing as needed.
4. **Verify early** — T+4h / T+24h pacing and attribution; escalate via [Ad Ops Troubleshooting](../05-ad-operations/troubleshooting.md) if needed.
5. **Hold In Testing** — Follow [In Testing → Complete checklist](../09-sop-library/checklists/in-testing-complete.md). Complete the parent task only after Incrementality Testing ends.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations

| Condition / Trigger | Escalation Path | Notification Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Zero impressions after 2 hours | Ad Ops Troubleshooting | Slack + Asana |
| Overpacing | Pause line item; Ad Ops | Slack + DSP |
| Take Live with open dependencies | Return to onboarding | Onboarding Overview |
