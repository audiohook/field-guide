# Client Onboarding

> **Department / Section**: 04-customer-success  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Director of Client Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: CSMs, CSEs, Creative, Billing / Affiliate specialists  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Contract Signed → Onboarding Overview  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-08

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Get a newly signed brand launch-ready on one parent task.
- **Definition of Done**: Onboarding call done; billing, tracking, creative, and account setup green; parent task at Take Live.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Onboarding call**: Within 3 business days of CSM assignment.
* **Take Live readiness**: All dependencies complete before flight start.
* **Cadence Check**: Weekly CS L10 on Onboarding Overview.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

Execute with the [Onboarding Stage Gates checklist](../09-sop-library/checklists/onboarding-gates.md).

1. **Own it** — Accept CSM ownership after [handoff](../03-sales/handoffs.md); do not create a second parent task.
2. **Set up the workspace** — Client Asana project + Drive folder; reply to Sales intro.
3. **Run the onboarding call** — Schedule, prepare, complete, document actions.
4. **Clear parallel dependencies** — Creative (via Creative Overview), tracking, billing/affiliate, UI config, Clarify.
5. **Gate Take Live** — Only when [Take-Live Readiness](../09-sop-library/checklists/take-live-readiness.md) is green → [Campaign Launch](./campaign-launch.md).

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations

| Condition / Trigger | Escalation Path | Notification Channel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Client silent > 5 business days | SAE re-engages | Slack + Asana |
| Tracking blocker | CSE / Ad Ops | Tracking subtask |
| Affiliate terms wrong (esp. reporting-only) | Affiliate specialist before Take Live | Billing/affiliate tasks |
| Missing payment method | Hold Take Live; Finance | Stripe + Asana |
| Client cancels | Canceled column + Clarify | Asana + Clarify |
