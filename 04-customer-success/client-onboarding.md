# Client Onboarding

> **Department / Section**: 04-customer-success  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Customer Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Customer Success Managers (CSMs)  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered Immediately After Sales Handoff  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Guide new advertisers through technical pixel implementation, creative ingestion, and campaign strategy alignment so their initial flight launches on time and error-free.
- **Definition of Done**: Kickoff call held, tracking pixel firing and verified, creative assets approved, and Ad Ops trafficking ticket submitted.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Time to First Launch**: Flight live within 10 business days of contract signature.
* **Pixel Verification Rate**: 100% of campaigns have verified pixel traffic before launch.
* **Kickoff Completion SLA**: Kickoff call held within 3 business days of signing.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Schedule Kickoff Call (Within 24 Hours)**: CSM sends kickoff booking link and onboarding questionnaire to client.
2. **Conduct Kickoff Call (30 mins)**:
   * Reaffirm campaign goals, CPA/ROAS targets, and target audience parameters.
   * Provide tracking pixel instructions or Shopify app install link.
   * Confirm creative asset delivery dates.
3. **Verify Tracking & Attribution**:
   * Inspect pixel firing in Audiohook Portal / conversions processor.
   * Verify UTM parameters and conversion event triggers (Purchase, Lead, Sign-up).
4. **Collect & QA Creative**:
   * Verify audio files meet technical specs (MP3/WAV, -16 to -14 LUFS, 15s/30s/60s).
   * Verify companion banner assets if applicable.
5. **Submit Trafficking Ticket to Ad Ops**:
   * Submit campaign build ticket in Linear with confirmed targeting parameters and approved creative.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Client unresponsive for > 5 business days: Escalate to AE to re-engage primary decision-maker.
* Technical pixel blocker: Schedule technical pairing session with Ad Ops / Engineering.
