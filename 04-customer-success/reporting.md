# Client Reporting & QBRs

> **Department / Section**: 04-customer-success  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Head of Customer Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: CSMs  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Weekly Updates, Mid-Flight Check-ins, Quarterly Reviews  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Clearly demonstrate business value, verified attribution, and strategic insights to retain clients and secure contract renewals/expansions.
- **Definition of Done**: Reports delivered on schedule, client questions answered, and QBR deck presented to decision-maker.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Reporting On-Time Delivery**: 100% of weekly reports sent by Tuesday 12:00 PM local time.
* **Net Revenue Retention (NRR)**: >= 115% quarterly NRR.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Weekly Performance Snapshot**:
   * Generate automated performance summary (Spend, Impressions, Conversions, CPA, ROAS, Top Shows).
   * Include 2–3 sentences of actionable insights and optimizations made.
2. **Mid-Flight Review Call**:
   * For flights > 60 days, conduct a 30-minute sync to present attribution lift, geographic insights, and creative performance.
3. **Quarterly Business Review (QBR)**:
   * Prepare executive review deck highlighting total ROI, halo effect, audience demographic data, and recommendations for the next quarter.
4. **Renewal & Expansion Discussion**:
   * Present proposed flight renewal and budget expansion 30 days prior to contract expiration.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Any client raising reporting discrepancies: Pair with Ad Ops and Data Engineering within 24 hours.
