# Restarting a Churned Client

> **Department / Section**: 04-customer-success  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Director of Client Success  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: CSMs, SAEs, Finance  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: A former client is being considered for re-engagement  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-10-14

The goal is not simply to restart the campaign. Decide whether the conditions that ended the relationship have changed enough that a successful partnership is now possible. Treat a restart as a new strategic partnership informed by the last one.

The full write-up is [Restarting a Churned Audiohook Client](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1keOlvf1aOxBiH0DKM6l0cykrRLXBLco_CnS-owqZDBo/edit).

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Decide whether a churned client should return, and define a Version 2 launch instead of recreating the old campaign.
- **Definition of Done**: Churn reason and "why now" are documented, fit and credit are re-checked, Sales, Client Success, Engineering, and Finance share the same picture, and a restart shape is chosen or the team decides not to re-engage.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Before outreach**: The churn reason is written down, categorized, and acknowledged in the conversation.
* **Before yes**: Finance has verified payment terms, outstanding balances, and credit status.
* **Restart plan**: 30-day success metrics, a 60-day optimization plan, and a reporting cadence are agreed before relaunch.

---

## 3. Core Steps (The 20/80 Flow)

1. **Why did they churn?** Document this before anyone reaches out. Ask whether they left because of performance, budget cuts, staffing, seasonality, tracking, attribution, affiliate platform limits, creative fatigue, product or pricing changes, an agency change, an acquisition, or because they were not ready. Categorize the reason as **External** (budget, layoffs, seasonality), **Operational** (tracking, onboarding, communication), **Performance** (CPA, volume, incrementality), or **Strategic** (channel priorities changed). The outreach names the original reason.
2. **Has anything materially changed?** On the client side, look for more traffic or conversions, new products, offers, leadership, agency, affiliate platform, tracking, first-party data, budget, or geographies. On Audiohook's side, look for product, optimization, inventory, reporting, integrations, attribution, creative, affiliate support, case studies, or onboarding changes. There needs to be a compelling "why now."
3. **Is this still a good-fit client?** Re-check traffic against minimums, whether conversion volume is enough to optimize, whether the CPA target is realistic, whether they understand incrementality, whether they can launch, and their credit and payment history (late payments, chargebacks, collections, contract issues). Sometimes the correct answer is not to re-engage.
4. **Review historical campaign data** before the first call. Performance: spend, conversions, CPA, trend, best creatives, publishers, inventory, and geographies. Operations: time to launch, tracking issues, support tickets, creative turnaround, and responsiveness. Client Success notes: personality, expectations, communication style, champions, and decision makers. Sales notes: procurement, legal, and pricing.
5. **Decide what the restart should look like.** Do not recreate the old campaign. Options include a small pilot (limited budget, short validation, one conversion event), a geographic rollout in the strongest historical markets, a new creative strategy, a different conversion event, a new inventory mix, a different attribution method if tracking improved, or a different pricing structure only if appropriate.
6. **Confirm internal readiness** before saying yes. Sales knows why they churned, why they are returning, and the new expectations. Client Success knows the previous pain points, success criteria, and risks. Engineering knows tracking and integration changes. Finance has verified payment terms, outstanding balances, and credit status.

---

## 4. Re-engagement conversation

**Business:** What prompted you to revisit Audiohook? What has changed? What are your marketing priorities? What is working, and what is missing?

**Performance:** How are other acquisition channels performing? What CPA are you targeting now? Has conversion volume changed? Has attribution changed?

**Technical:** Any website, GA4, tracking, affiliate platform, or conversion-event changes?

**Organizational:** Is the decision-making team the same? Who owns the relationship? Has agency support changed?

**Expectations:** What would success look like this time? What would make you stop again? What concerns do you still have?

Use **Stop, Change, Start**:

* **Stop** — What in the last engagement created friction or failed to deliver value?
* **Change** — What strategies, processes, expectations, or technical approaches change based on what both sides learned?
* **Start** — What capabilities, reporting, creative, optimization, or communication were not part of the original relationship?

---

## 5. Checklist before relaunch

**Commercial:** Previous contract reviewed. Outstanding invoices resolved. Current pricing approved. New IO prepared. Billing method confirmed.

**Technical:** GA4 connection verified. Tracking validated. Conversion events confirmed. Affiliate platform configured. Universal tag reviewed, if applicable.

**Campaign:** Creative reviewed. Messaging updated. New promotions considered. Geography, budget, CPA, and launch goals confirmed.

**Success planning:** 30-day success metrics documented. 60-day optimization plan set. Creative roadmap prepared. Reporting cadence agreed. Key stakeholders identified.
