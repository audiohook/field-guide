# 05. Ad Operations

This section details technical campaign delivery, creative trafficking, inventory management, pixel tracking verification, and DSP troubleshooting at Audiohook.

**Ad Ops is a function of RevOps, not a department.** It reports through Sales & RevOps (Carrie), alongside Sales and Customer Success. These process docs live here for readability; they do not represent a separate department on the [Org Chart](../01-company/org-chart.md).

---

## Documents & Core Processes

* **[Campaign Setup & Trafficking](./campaign-setup.md)**: Line item configuration, flight dates, bid strategies, and budget caps in the Audiohook DSP.
* **[Creative Management](./creative.md)**: Audio asset specifications, loudness normalization (LUFS), companion banners, and CDN hosting.
* **[Tracking & Pixel Implementation](./tracking.md)**: Web tracking pixels, Shopify server-to-server integration, mobile app attribution, and UTM parameters.
* **[Campaign QA Checklist](./qa.md)**: Mandatory pre-flight quality assurance review to prevent trafficking errors.
* **[Operational Troubleshooting](./troubleshooting.md)**: Underpacing, exchange bid rejections, pixel firing dropoffs, and discrepancy resolution.
