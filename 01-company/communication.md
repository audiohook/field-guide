# Communication Norms

> **Department / Section**: 01-company

> **Trigger / Cadence**: Daily Practice  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Provide clear rules of engagement for internal communication to minimize noise, preserve focus, and ensure transparent collaboration.
- **Definition of Done**: Team members use the correct communication channel for each type of message with agreed-upon response expectations.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Asynchronous SLA**: Non-urgent Slack messages answered within 4 hours during working hours.
* **Urgent Paging SLA**: Critical system alerts acknowledged within on-call targets (< 15 mins for S1).

---

## 3. Communication Channels & Rules of Engagement

| Channel | Primary Purpose | Urgency / SLA | Do's & Don'ts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Slack** | Team coordination, daily updates, quick questions | Semi-sync (respond within ~4 hours) | • Use public channels over DMs<br>• Use threads to keep channels tidy<br>• Avoid `@here` / `@channel` unless truly urgent |
| **Linear** | Engineering, product, and handbook task tracking | Asynchronous (daily) | • All engineering work must have a Linear issue<br>• Update status and blockers directly on issues |
| **GitHub** | Code reviews, process documentation reviews | Asynchronous (within 24 hours) | • Review PRs within 1 business day<br>• Use constructive, actionable feedback |
| **Email** | External client/partner communication, formal contracts | Asynchronous | • Keep internal discussion out of email threads |
| **Zoom / Huddles** | L10 meetings, complex pairing, sprint planning | Real-time | • Always have an agenda<br>• Keep meetings tight and on schedule |

---

## 4. Default to Public & Async
1. **Public Over Private**: Default to posting in public channels (`#general`, `#dev`, `#sales`, `#ops`) rather than 1:1 DMs so context is preserved and shared.
2. **Bad News Travels Fast**: Flag blockers, client dissatisfaction, or technical outages immediately without sugarcoating.
3. **Protect Deep Work**: Respect notification statuses and focus hours.
