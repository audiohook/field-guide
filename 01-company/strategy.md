# Strategy & Target Market

> **Department / Section**: 01-company  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Visionary / Head of Sales  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Sales, Marketing, Product, Leadership  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Reviewed Quarterly at EOS Planning  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Define Audiohook's target market, unique differentiators, and strategic priorities.
- **Definition of Done**: Sales prospects, product roadmaps, and marketing campaigns strictly align with our Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **ICP Win Rate**: >= 30% close rate on verified ICP leads.
* **Gross Margin / Unit Economics**: Sustainable unit economics per campaign flight.

---

## 3. Core Framework (The 20/80)
1. **Target Market (The Demographic, Geographic, Psychographic Profile)**:
   * Direct-to-Consumer (DTC) brands and agencies spending $10k+/month in digital audio/podcast media.
   * High performance sensitivity (CPA/ROAS focus vs. vanity reach metrics).
2. **Our Three Uniques**:
   * *1. Proprietary DSP & Bidding Tech*: Purpose-built for digital audio precision.
   * *2. Deterministic Attribution & Measurement*: Verifiable audio ROI (e.g. Verified Listens, IP/geo conversion matching).
   * *3. High-Touch Operational Partnership*: Dedicated campaign management and optimization support.
3. **Proven Process**: The standardized customer journey from initial discovery to renewal and scale.
4. **Guarantee**: [To be documented by Leadership]

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Non-ICP deals requesting custom feature development must be reviewed and approved by the Product & Sales leads before a contract is generated.
