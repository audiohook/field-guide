# How We Work (EOS Cadence)

> **Department / Section**: 01-company  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Integrator  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: Leadership & All Department Leads  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Weekly (L10), Quarterly, Annually  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Maintain operational traction, accountability, and issue resolution through standard EOS cadences.
- **Definition of Done**: Weekly L10 meetings run on time, scorecards are green, and quarterly rocks achieve >= 80% completion.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Weekly L10 Rating**: Average meeting rating >= 8/10.
* **To-Do Completion Rate**: >= 90% of weekly to-dos completed within 7 days.
* **Quarterly Rock Completion**: >= 80% completed per quarter.

---

## 3. Core Operating Cadences (The 20/80 Flow)

### 1. Weekly Level 10 (L10) Meeting (90 Minutes)
* **Segue (5 mins)**: Personal and professional good news.
* **Scorecard Review (5 mins)**: Review 5–15 weekly metrics; report on or off track without discussion.
* **Rock Review (5 mins)**: Review quarterly rocks (on or off track).
* **Customer / Employee Headlines (5 mins)**: Brief positive or negative headlines.
* **To-Do List (5 mins)**: Review previous week's to-dos (done / not done).
* **IDS — Identify, Discuss, Solve (60 mins)**: Prioritize issues (1, 2, 3), dig to the root cause, and agree on action items.
* **Conclude (5 mins)**: Recap to-dos, rate meeting (1–10).

### 2. Quarterly EOS Planning (Full Day)
* Review previous quarter's rocks and financials.
* Reset company and department rocks for the upcoming 90 days.
* Resolve key strategic issues on the company issues list.

### 3. Annual Strategic Planning (2 Days)
* Review and update the Vision/Traction Organizer (V/TO).
* Confirm 3-Year Picture and 1-Year Plan.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* Any scorecard metric or quarterly rock off track for 2 consecutive weeks must automatically be dropped down into the weekly L10 Issues List for IDS.
