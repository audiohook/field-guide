# Hiring Philosophy

> **Department / Section**: 01-company  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Integrator / Leadership Team  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All Hiring Managers & Interviewers  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered on New Role Requisition  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Maintain an uncompromising hiring bar that ensures every addition to the Audiohook team aligns with our Core Values, fits their EOS Accountability seat, and raises the team's talent density.
- **Definition of Done**: Candidate passes all core value screens, scorecards match role expectations, and formal leadership approval is secured before an offer is extended.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **First-Year Retention Rate**: >= 85% of new hires retained and performing well.
* **Right People, Right Seat (RPRS)**: 100% of new hires rated "+" on all Core Values after 90 days.
* **Time-to-Productive**: New hires shipping code or managing accounts within 30 days.

---

## 3. Core Hiring Principles (The 20/80 Flow)

### 1. The "WHO" Framework
We hire for specific outcomes and competencies, not resumes. Every new role must have a **Job Scorecard** defining:
* The mission of the role.
* The 3–5 quantifiable business outcomes expected within the first 12 months.
* The critical competencies and cultural traits required.

### 2. Core Values Fit (The Non-Negotiable Filter)
Skills can be taught; core values cannot. We screen ruthlessly for:
* Extreme ownership and proactive problem-solving.
* Speed, simplicity, and low ego.
* Transparency and direct, blameless communication.
* High curiosity and continuous craft improvement.

### 3. The Interview Funnel
1. **Resume & Intake Screen (30 mins)**: Recruiter or hiring manager verifies baseline qualifications, compensation alignment, and motivation.
2. **Hiring Manager Deep Dive (45 mins)**: Structured interview walking through career history, achievements, and technical or functional competencies.
3. **Practical Skills Assessment**: Realistic, practical work-sample test (e.g., technical pairing, sales pitch simulation, or case review). No generic whiteboard puzzles.
4. **Core Values & Peer Screen (45 mins)**: Evaluation with cross-functional peers specifically grading cultural alignment using the EOS People Analyzer.
5. **Reference Checks**: At least 2 past managers contacted to verify past performance and work ethic.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* **Offer Approval**: No offer may be extended without written approval from the Hiring Manager and the Integrator.
* **Split Decisions**: If any interviewer marks a candidate as a culture risk ("-" on Core Values), the candidate is a "No Hire" by default.
