# Company Onboarding

> **Department / Section**: 01-company  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Integrator / Operations Lead  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All New Hires & Hiring Managers  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Triggered on New Hire Start Date  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Integrate new team members rapidly, ensure complete alignment with Audiohook's culture and EOS cadence, and empower them to make meaningful contributions in their first 30 days.
- **Definition of Done**: New hire completes system setup, reads the company handbook, meets key team partners, and completes their 30-60-90 day milestone review.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Handbook Orientation Completion**: 100% of new hires complete the Handbook walkthrough within Week 1.
* **First Meaningful Output**: First PR merged, campaign launched, or prospect contacted within Day 14.
* **30-Day Check-in Rating**: >= 9/10 positive onboarding satisfaction rating.

---

## 3. Core Onboarding Flow (The 20/80 Flow)

### Week 1: Foundations & The Handbook
1. **System & Tool Access (Day 1)**: IT provisioning (Google Workspace, 1Password, Slack, GitHub, Linear, HubSpot, AWS).
2. **Company Handbook Orientation (Days 1–3)**:
   * Read the [Company Mission & Values](./mission.md) and [How We Work](./how-we-work.md).
   * Review the [Leadership Guide](../LEADERSHIP_GUIDE.md) to understand how processes are documented and edited in GitHub.
   * Review the [Building Production Tools with AI](./building-production-tools-with-ai.md) policy.
3. **Welcome & Culture 1:1**: Kickoff conversation with manager and assigned onboarding buddy.

### Month 1 (Days 1–30): Department Immersion
1. **Department Core Process Training**: Deep-dive into department-specific processes (e.g., Sales, Ad Ops, Engineering).
2. **Shadowing**: Shadow 3–5 real customer calls, campaign setups, or sprint deployments.
3. **First Milestone Deliverable**: Complete first self-directed project or ticket.
4. **Day 30 Review**: Structured check-in between employee and manager to review role scorecard alignment.

### Months 2 & 3 (Days 31–90): Independence & Ownership
1. **Autonomous Execution**: Manage core responsibilities with minimal supervision.
2. **Quarterly Rock Assignment**: Participate in the upcoming quarter's EOS planning and take ownership of department rocks.
3. **Day 90 People Analyzer Review**: Mutual review of Core Values and GWC (Gets it, Wants it, Capacity).

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* If a new hire shows critical misalignment on Core Values or GWC during their first 30 days, schedule an immediate alignment session with the department head and Integrator.
