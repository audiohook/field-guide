# Core Values

> **Department / Section**: 01-company  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Integrator / Leadership Team  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All Audiohook Team Members  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Integrated into Daily Work, Hiring, and Quarterly Reviews  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Articulate the non-negotiable behavioral traits and decision-making filters of the Audiohook team.
- **Definition of Done**: Core values are used actively in hiring screens, quarterly performance evaluations, and team recognition.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **People Analyzer Alignment**: >= 85% "+ / +" ratings across the team in quarterly reviews.
* **Value-Based Recognition**: Regular peer nominations in team retrospectives.

---

## 3. Core Values & Behaviors
*[To be finalized and documented with the Leadership Team]*

1. **Get Shit Done**: At the end of the day, we judge by the work accomplished.
2. **Focus on Speed & Simplicity**: Choose clear, simple solutions over complicated bureaucracy. Ship, learn, and iterate.
3. **Transparent & Direct Communication**: Give blameless, honest feedback early. Bad news travels fast.
4. **Continuous Craft Improvement**: Stay curious, master your tools (including AI), and raise the quality bar every quarter.

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* When team members consistently violate core values, issue a "3-strike" PIP conversation guided by the People Analyzer framework in [Performance Reviews](../02-people/performance.md).
