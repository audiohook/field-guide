# Mission & Vision

> **Department / Section**: 01-company  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Visionary / Integrator  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All Audiohook Team Members  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Reviewed Annually at Strategic Planning  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-17

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Define the fundamental reason Audiohook exists, the future state we are building toward, and the North Star that aligns all team decisions.
- **Definition of Done**: Every team member can articulate why we exist and how their role directly contributes to our mission.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Company Vision Alignment**: 100% of team members aligned on company mission in quarterly surveys.
* **Annual Goal Progress**: Company rock completion rate >= 80%.

---

## 3. Core Narrative (The 20/80)
1. **Core Purpose**: Audiohook empowers brands and agencies to achieve measurable, performance-driven results through modern audio advertising.
2. **10-Year Target (BHAG)**: 1,000,000 leveraging the Audiohook platform.
3. **Core Focus**:
   * *Niche*: Programmatic audio demand-side platform and attribution measurement.
   * *Passion*: Building high-performance, transparent audio advertising infrastructure.
4. **Annual & 3-Year Picture**: Outlined in the EOS Vision/Traction Organizer (V/TO).

---

## 4. Exceptions & Escalations
* If strategic initiatives or partner opportunities appear misaligned with our core focus, escalate to the Leadership Team during the weekly Level 10 meeting for IDS (Identify, Discuss, Solve).
