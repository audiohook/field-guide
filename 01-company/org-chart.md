# Org Chart & Accountability

> **Department / Section**: 01-company  
> **Process Owner (Accountability Seat)**: Integrator  
> **Target Audience (Who Follows It)**: All Employees  
> **Trigger / Cadence**: Updated Whenever Roles Change; Reviewed Quarterly  
> **Last Reviewed**: 2026-09-18

---

## 1. Purpose & Outcome (Definition of Done)
- **Purpose**: Clearly define every seat in the organization, its 5 core roles, and who sits in each seat using the EOS Accountability Chart.
- **Definition of Done**: 100% of seats have an owner, clear GWC (Gets it, Wants it, Capacity to do it), and no overlapping or orphan responsibilities.

---

## 2. Key Measurables (KPIs)
* **Right People, Right Seats (RPRS)**: >= 90% seat fit across all departments.
* **Role Clarity**: Zero role confusion escalations in quarterly reviews.

## 4. GWC Standards
Every team member must:
* **G (Gets it)**: Truly understands the role, the pace, and the business context.
* **W (Wants it)**: Genuinely loves doing the work and is motivated to excel.
* **C (Capacity to do it)**: Has the intellectual, physical, and emotional capability and time.
